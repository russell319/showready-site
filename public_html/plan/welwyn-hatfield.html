Event licences and notice periods in Welwyn Hatfield

Licensing for events in Welwyn Hatfield is handled by Welwyn Hatfield Council.

What you needNotice
Temporary Event Notice (alcohol, music or late food, under 500 people)At least 10 working days before, £21, given to Welwyn Hatfield Council
Event notification to the councilCheck Welwyn Hatfield Council’s events page; many councils ask for 8 to 12 weeks for public events
Road closureUsually 8 to 12 weeks before, from the highways authority

    First aid, medical cover and the ambulance service in Welwyn Hatfield

    Events in Welwyn Hatfield are covered by the East of England Ambulance Service (EEAST).

    • Tell the ambulance service: There’s no online form. The council’s Safety Advisory Group passes your medical plan to EEAST. EEAST website
    • Tell the council: start with the events or licensing team on Welwyn Hatfield Council’s website (find it on GOV.UK). Its Safety Advisory Group looks at your medical plan with the ambulance service.
    • Who can provide cover: In England, first aid needs no registration, but anything beyond first aid (paramedics, nurses or doctors treating people, or a private ambulance) must come from a provider registered with the Care Quality Commission. This is enforced from 6 December 2027.
    • What they’ll ask for: a medical needs assessment, a medical plan written by your provider, cover from build-up to break-down, and how an ambulance reaches the site (the Purple Guide medical chapter).
