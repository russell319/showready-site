Special places and clearances

Locations

    Risk assessments

    One for each location, started for you from what happens there. Check each hazard, add your own controls, rate what’s left and sign it off. Film offices, councils and landowners usually ask for it with your application.

    Permit tracker

    Costs and deposits

    Location fees, council and police charges, security, deposits and overtime, by location and for the whole shoot.

    Crew catering

    Feed the unit at each location: headcount, meals and dietary needs, a budget that goes straight into Costs, and local caterers on ShowReady you can ask for a quote.

    Unit vehicles and accommodation

    Artist trailers, motorhomes, honeywagons, welfare units and crew rooms for each location: put them on the unit base plan, and ask ShowReady suppliers nearby for a quote.

    Unit base plans

    Draw each location's unit base on the aerial photo, to scale: tech trucks, honeywagons, catering, crew parking, camera positions, cable runs and emergency access.

    Call sheets

    A call sheet for every shoot day. Add the times, scenes, cast and contacts; ShowReady fills in the location, unit base, weather, sun times, nearest A&E, police, catering, permits and safety notes, then makes the PDF.
