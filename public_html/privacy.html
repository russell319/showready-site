The short version

  • Without an account, your event plans, quotes, tracker and enquiries are saved only in your own browser. We don't receive them.
  • If you create an account, we store your email, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password and the plans or supplier desk you save, so you can use them on any device. You can delete your account and everything in it yourself, at any time.
  • If you join as a founding member, we keep the details you give us so we can contact you about the pilot.
  • We don't sell your information. If you use ShowReady free, you may see a Google advert along the bottom of the page; Google asks your permission before using cookies for adverts. Paid plans and founding members see no adverts.

Supplier profiles, enquiries and messages

If you're a supplier and choose to show your profile, anyone using ShowReady can see your business name, town, services, the events you work, your description, review links and whether your insurance is in date. Your address, phone numbers and contact email are never shown on your profile. You can hide your profile at any time.

When an organiser sends you an enquiry, they share their name and any phone or email they choose. When you reply, that organiser can see your business phone numbers and contact email. Enquiries and messages are kept in your account so both of you have a record, and are only visible to the two of you. They're deleted if either of you deletes your account (the other person's copy of their own messages goes too). We may look at a conversation only if one of you reports a problem.

When you get a new enquiry, message or quote, we email you to say so (never the message itself). You can turn these emails off in Account.

If you draw a site plan, it's saved with your event plan. If you send it with an enquiry, that supplier can see it.

Documents in your Event folder

If you're signed in, you can upload documents to your Event folder, such as an insurance certificate, a signed risk assessment, suppliers' paperwork or letters from the council. We store them privately with Supabase, on servers in London, so you can open them on any device. Only you can see them: we don't share them with councils, suppliers or partners, and we don't look at them unless you ask us to help with a problem. ShowReady never submits anything to a council for you; you download your documents and send them yourself.

The legal basis is contract (providing the service you asked for). We keep each document until you delete it, delete the event plan it belongs to, or delete your account, which removes the files as well. Uploads may contain other people's names and contact details (for example a supplier's certificate): only upload what you're entitled to share, and only what the council or landowner needs.

Information stored in your browser

The planner and Supplier desk save your work in your browser's local storage so it's there when you come back. If you sign in, local storage also keeps you signed in on that device. If you use or install ShowReady as an app, your browser also keeps a copy of the pages you've opened so they load when you're offline; it holds page files only, never your account details. This is needed for the service you asked for, so no consent banner is required. Unless you're signed in, nothing is sent to us. Only people with your password can see what's saved in your account: it's locked to you in our database. Anyone else using the same browser on the same device could see it, so on a shared computer use “Sign out and clear this device”, or clear your browser data.

Insurance partners

If you list your firm as an insurance partner, we keep the details you enter and your account email to run your listing and contact you about it. We show your firm's name, FCA details, description and contact details to organisers and suppliers whose events or trades you cover. When someone clicks a partner's website, phone or email button, we count it anonymously (just the partner, the button, where it was and when) so partners can see how their listing is used. No details of the person clicking are kept or passed on. This is in our legitimate interest in running the listings, and the counts are kept for up to 2 years.

Counting visits from council links

When you arrive from a council's website, a council email or a link on its event application system (pages like beshowready.co.uk/plan/bristol), we count anonymously how many visits came from each council and how many went on to start a plan, download an export pack or open the council's application form. Each count holds only the council, where the link was placed, the step and a random number that lasts for that browser tab. It includes no names, emails, IP addresses or cookies, and it can't be linked to you or your plan. We use it to see whether the links help, in our legitimate interest in running the service, and keep it for up to 2 years. Arriving through these links also hides adverts and supplier promotion for that visit.

To stop people copying our council information in bulk, each time a council page is looked up we keep a scrambled code made from your IP address. It can't be turned back into the address, the scrambling changes every day, and the codes are deleted after 2 days. It's used only to limit how many councils one visitor can look up, in our legitimate interest in protecting the service.
