ShowReady Insurers and brokers

Be there when organisers sort their insurance

ShowReady is a free planner for UK events. When an organiser plans a fete, a bonfire night or a festival, it shows them the cover they need and what to tell their insurer. Listed insurers and brokers whose cover fits that event appear right there, and organisers contact you directly.

How it works

  • Matched to the event. Organisers see you only when you cover their activities (bonfires, fireworks, inflatables, rides, animals and more), their event size and their area.
  • You do the selling. Organisers see your name, FCA statement, description and your website, phone and email buttons. ShowReady doesn’t recommend, rank or compare firms, and never passes on organisers’ details.
  • Suppliers too. If you insure trades such as caterers, inflatable hirers or marquee firms, the specialist plan also lists you on their supplier desk.
  • Checked first. We check your firm on the FCA register before your listing goes live.
  • Your numbers. Once you’re live, this page shows how many times organisers clicked your buttons.
