Cancellations and refunds

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains how to cancel a paid ShowReady plan and when you can get your money back. It forms part of our terms of use. Nothing in this policy affects your legal rights.

1. During the pilot

Everything on ShowReady is free during the pilot, which runs until 1 February 2027, so there's nothing to cancel or refund. Founding members and anyone on a free period (for example through a trade association offer) aren't charged until that free period ends, and we'll email you before your first payment is due.

2. Cancelling a monthly or yearly plan

  • You can cancel at any time from Manage billing on your Supplier desk or account, or by emailing us.
  • Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you've already paid for, then stops. You won't be charged again.
  • We don't refund part of a month or year you've already started, except as set out below or where the law says we must.

3. Your 14-day right to cancel

If you're buying as a consumer (for yourself, not for a business), you can cancel within 14 days of buying a plan or Event pass and get a refund. If you've started using paid features in those 14 days, we may keep a fair amount for what you've used, worked out by the days used. Business customers don't have this legal right, but see section 4.

4. Yearly renewals

We email a reminder before a yearly plan renews. If it renews and you didn't mean it to, contact us within 14 days of the renewal date. If you haven't used paid features since it renewed, we'll refund the renewal in full and cancel the plan. This applies to business customers as well as consumers.

5. Event pass

An Event pass is a one-off payment that unlocks the extra tools for one event for 12 months. You can get a full refund within 14 days if you haven't used the extra tools yet. Once they've been used, the pass isn't refundable, including if your event is cancelled or postponed, but you can move the pass to a new date or a different event by emailing us.

6. When something goes wrong on our side

  • If you're charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or charged after cancelling, we'll refund the extra in full.
  • If a paid feature you rely on doesn't work for a significant time and we can't fix it, we'll refund the affected period or offer a free extension, whichever you prefer.
  • If we close ShowReady or withdraw a paid plan, we'll refund any time you've paid for and not used.

7. Enterprise plans

Enterprise plans are priced by quote. Cancellation and refunds follow the terms in your quote or agreement, and this policy applies where those terms say nothing different.

8. Deals between organisers and suppliers

ShowReady doesn't take payments or commission for work agreed between organisers and suppliers, so we can't refund deposits, pitch fees or hire charges. Those are between you and the other party, under the terms you agree with them.

9. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@beshowready.co.uk from the email address on your account, with the plan and the date you paid. We'll reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the card or account you paid with within 14 days; your bank may take another 5 to 10 working days to show it. Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see your full card number.

If you're unhappy with a payment, please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank. We'll always try to sort it out quickly.
