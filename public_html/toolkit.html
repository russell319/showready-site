Tools and pricing

Everything an organiser needs, built for you from one description

You see as much or as little of this as you want: start with the essentials and switch more on when you need it. The jobs that usually mean council websites, blank spreadsheets and quotes lost in your inbox are done for you, kept in one place, and updated the moment your plans change.

  • Plan from a description

    Type it the way you'd tell a friend. ShowReady picks out the numbers, dates, alcohol, music and site.

  • Licences and contacts

    Which licences apply, why, and the council, police and highways teams to contact, with links to apply.

  • Every deadline worked out

    Counted back from your date, including the working-day rule for Temporary Event Notices.

  • Planning spreadsheet

    Download an Excel workbook with a colour timeline, busy-week warnings, vendor money with live formulas and your risk assessment.

  • Draft risk assessment

    Hazards, who could be harmed and the controls, built from your event details and ready for sign-off.

  • Supplier quotes side by side

    Every supplier you've asked, what they quoted and what's included, with accepted quotes ticked off for you.

  • Vendor money calculator

    Hire fee, pitch fee, % of takings or both: see what you'll pay or earn from food and drink vendors before you agree.

  • Tracker and readiness score

    Tick off every task, see what's overdue, and watch the event reach 100% ready.

  • Site plan on a map

    Draw your site to scale over a street map or aerial photo: stage, pitches, toilets, fire points and routes. Print it or send it with an enquiry.

  • Council application pack

    Your answers laid out the way council event forms ask for them, including EventApp, ready to copy across, plus your site plan as a PDF.

  • Find suppliers and message them

    Search suppliers near your event who offer what you need, send an enquiry and keep the conversation in one place.
