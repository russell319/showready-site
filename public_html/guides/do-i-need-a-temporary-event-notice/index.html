Do I need a Temporary Event Notice (TEN)?

By Russell Bowers, ShowReady · Last checked 8 November 2026 · England and Wales

Short answer

You need a Temporary Event Notice if you sell alcohol, put on regulated entertainment (such as amplified music) for the public, or serve hot food or drink between 11pm and 5am, at a place that has no premises licence covering it. A TEN covers up to 499 people at once (staff included) for up to 168 hours. Give it to the council at least 10 working days before the event. It costs £21.

What counts as licensable

Under the Licensing Act 2003 three kinds of activity need a licence or a TEN:

  • selling alcohol, including alcohol included in a ticket price or a “free” drink with paid entry;
  • regulated entertainment for the public, such as amplified live music, recorded music, plays, films and boxing or wrestling (there are exemptions, see our music guide);
  • hot food or hot drink sold between 11pm and 5am.

Free drinks at a private party that nobody pays to attend are not usually licensable. Charging in any form changes that.

The day the council receives the notice and the first day of the event don’t count towards the 10 working days.

Who can object

Only the police and the council’s environmental health team can object to a TEN. If they object to a standard TEN the council holds a hearing; an objection to a late TEN means the event can’t go ahead with licensable activities.

When you need more than a TEN

If 500 or more people will be there at once, or the event runs longer than 7 days, you need a premises licence instead. That takes a 28-day consultation, so allow several months. Larger public events are also usually asked to go to the council’s Safety Advisory Group.

Common questions

How far in advance do I need to submit a TEN?

At least 10 clear working days before the event. A late TEN can be given 5 to 9 working days before, but a single objection from the police or environmental health stops it.

How many people can a Temporary Event Notice cover?

Up to 499 people on site at any one time, and that includes staff, volunteers and performers.

How much does a TEN cost?

The fee is £21, paid to the council where the event takes place.

How many TENs can I give in a year?

Five a year if you don’t hold a personal licence, or 50 if you do. A single premises can have 15 TENs a year, covering no more than 21 days in total.
