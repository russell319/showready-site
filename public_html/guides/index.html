Event planning guides for England and Wales

By Russell Bowers · Last checked 8 November 2026

In short

Most small community events need landowner permission, insurance and a risk assessment. Alcohol and amplified music usually need a Temporary Event Notice, and bigger events, road closures and fireworks bring in the council’s events team. These guides explain each one.

  • Do I need a Temporary Event Notice (TEN)?

    When an event in England or Wales needs a Temporary Event Notice, the 499-person limit, 10 working days’ notice, the £21 fee and how many TENs you can give.

  • What licences does a village fete or summer fair need?

    A checklist of the licences and permissions a village fete, school fair or community event usually needs in England and Wales, and how much notice each takes.

  • What is a Safety Advisory Group (SAG) and does my event need one?

    What a council Safety Advisory Group does, which events usually go to one, what to send and how much notice to give.

  • How do I get a road closed for an event or street party?

    How temporary road closures for events work in England and Wales, the law councils use, typical notice periods and what you’ll be asked for.

  • Bonfire and fireworks rules for community events

    The firework curfew, what you need for a bonfire or fireworks display at a public event, safety distances and who to tell.

  • Do I need public liability insurance for an event?

    Whether event organisers need public liability insurance, how much cover councils and venues ask for, employers’ liability, and what to tell your insurer.

  • Do I need a licence for a raffle or tombola at an event?

    When a raffle or tombola at a fete, fair or community event needs no licence, the £500 prize and £100 expenses limits, and when you must register a small society lottery.

  • Do I need a licence for music at a community event?

    When live or recorded music at a fete, fair or community event needs a TEN or premises licence, the exemptions, and the separate music copyright licence.
