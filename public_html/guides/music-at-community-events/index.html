Do I need a licence for music at a community event?

By Russell Bowers, ShowReady · Last checked 8 November 2026 · England and Wales

Short answer

Unamplified live music needs no entertainment licence anywhere between 8am and 11pm. Amplified live music and recorded music for up to 500 people are exempt between 8am and 11pm in some places, such as pubs while open for alcohol and community halls with the owner’s consent. On a field, street or park that isn’t covered, amplified music for the public usually needs a Temporary Event Notice. Playing copyright music also usually needs TheMusicLicence from PPL PRS.

Two separate permissions

PermissionWhat it’s forFrom
Entertainment licence (TEN or premises licence)Putting on regulated entertainment for the publicThe council
TheMusicLicencePaying the songwriters and performers whose music is playedPPL PRS

One doesn’t replace the other. A venue may already hold both, so ask.

When music is exempt from licensing

  • Unamplified live music: no licence between 8am and 11pm, any audience size, anywhere.
  • Amplified live music and recorded music to an audience of up to 500, between 8am and 11pm, in premises licensed to sell alcohol while open for that.
  • Amplified live and recorded music for up to 500 people, 8am to 11pm, in community premises such as village halls and church halls, and on local authority, school and hospital premises, with the consent of whoever is responsible for them.

Outside these, for example amplified music on a privately owned field at a public event, plan on a TEN for under 500 people.

Noise

Amplified music outdoors usually needs a noise plan agreed with the council’s environmental health team, often as a condition of the licence. Tell nearby residents and finish at a sensible time.

TheMusicLicence for charities and community groups

PPL PRS offers a discount (not an exemption) for charity and community events that meet at least two of: free entry, the charitable purpose is advertised, and it’s run by volunteers. Apply at least 14 days before the event.

Common questions

Do I need a licence for a band at a village fete?

If the band is amplified and the fete is on land that isn’t covered by an exemption, you usually need a Temporary Event Notice. An unamplified band between 8am and 11pm needs no entertainment licence.

Do I need a music licence for a charity event?

Usually yes for copyright: TheMusicLicence from PPL PRS. Charity and community events can get a discount if they meet at least two of the conditions (free entry, advertised charitable purpose, run by volunteers).

Is a DJ at a private party licensable?

Not usually, if the party is private, not open to the public and nobody pays to attend.
